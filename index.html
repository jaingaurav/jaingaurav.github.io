About

I'm an engineer who has spent two decades building software at every layer of the stack — Bluetooth firmware on the original iPhone at Apple, Linux kernel tracing at Facebook, cloud data management as a founding engineer at Rubrik, and TensorFlow and applied ML at Google Brain. Today I'm a Principal Engineer at Snowflake, building the secure sandbox and agent infrastructure behind Cortex and Snowpark.

I studied computer science at the University of Waterloo (BMath and MASc), hold patents spanning distributed systems, databases, and wireless networking, and contribute to open source projects including Diamond, Pygments, and Wine.

Away from the keyboard I play piano, sing, lift weights, and take on home improvement projects.
